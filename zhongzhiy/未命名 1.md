## **Speech: The Final Letter of April**

### **I. Introduction: The Significance of Today (1 min)**

**Hook:** Good morning, everyone. Do you know what day it is?

Today is **April 30th**. For most people, it’s just the end of another month. But for fans of the masterpiece _Your Lie in April_, today marks the end of a journey. It is the day when the spring breeze carries away the last cherry blossoms, and a long-hidden secret finally reaches its destination.

Today, I want to talk about this story—not just as an anime, but as a lesson on how to live, how to love, and how to find color in a world that often feels grey.

---

### **II. The Boy in Monotone (1 min)**

The story begins with Kousei Arima. He was a piano prodigy, a "Human Metronome" who played with mechanical perfection. But after losing his mother, he lost his ability to "hear" the notes. To him, the world was **monotone**—a flat, silent, black-and-white landscape.

He wasn't just deaf to music; he was deaf to life. He was a soul trapped at the bottom of a dark ocean. Many of us have felt like Kousei at some point—stuck in a routine, losing passion, or feeling like the "colors" of our dreams have faded away.

---

### **III. The Girl of Color & The "Lie" (1.5 mins)**

Then came Kaori Miyazono. She was a violinist who broke every rule. She didn't care about the judges; she cared about the audience. She didn't just play music; she _lived_ it.

She "lied" her way into Kousei’s life. She pretended to be in love with his best friend just to get close to him. Why? Because she knew her time was running out. She was terminally ill, but instead of fading away in silence, she chose to burn like a firework.

Kaori dragged Kousei back to the stage. She taught him that **"Music is freedom."** Through her, we learn that the people we meet are like chemical reactions—they change our composition forever. Because of Kaori, Kousei’s world turned from monochrome to a vibrant, chaotic, and beautiful masterpiece.

---

### **IV. The Ending: A Journey Beyond April (1 min)**

As we reach the end of April today, we remember the finale of the story. Kaori passes away, leaving behind a single letter. That letter revealed the "Lie"—she had loved Kousei all along, since they were children.

It’s a heartbreaking ending, but it isn’t a tragedy. It’s a story of **legacy**. Even though Kaori is gone, her music lives on through Kousei’s fingers. She changed him. She saved him.

---

### **V. Conclusion: What is Your Spring? (30 sec)**

As we head into May tomorrow, ask yourself: Who are the people who bring color to your life? And more importantly, whose world are _you_ brightening?

_Your Lie in April_ tells us that life is short and often unfair. But as Kaori said: **"Whether you’re sad, a mess, or hit rock bottom, you still have to play!"** The month of April is ending today, but the courage to live passionately should never end.

Thank you.

### **演讲小贴士 (Performance Tips):**

1. **语速控制：** 开头讲日期时可以稍慢，营造一种“仪式感”。提到 Kaori 的活力时语速加快，显得轻快活泼；讲到结局和信件时，语速降下来，声音低沉一点。
    
2. **视觉辅助 (PPT建议)：**
    
    - **Slide 1:** 今天的日期 (April 30th) 和 满园樱花。
        
    - **Slide 2:** 只有黑白琴键的公生。
        
    - **Slide 3:** 站在舞台上、光芒四射的薰。
        
    - **Slide 4:** 最后一封信的画面。
        
3. **互动：** 讲到 "The world was monotone" 时，可以环视一下教室，停顿 2 秒，让大家感受到那种压抑感。
