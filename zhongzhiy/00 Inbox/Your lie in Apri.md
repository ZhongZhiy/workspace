

## English Speech: The April Lie and the Color of Life

### I. Introduction (0:00 - 1:00)

**Good morning, everyone.**

Spring has finally arrived. When we look at the cherry blossoms drifting in the wind, what comes to your mind? For many anime fans, April isn’t just a month of new beginnings; it’s a season that carries a beautiful yet heartbreaking memory. Today, I want to share with you one of the most touching stories I’ve ever encountered: **_Your Lie in April_** (or _Shigatsu wa Kimi no Uso_).

As we are standing in the middle of April right now, there is no better time to talk about this masterpiece.

### II. The Story of Gray and Color (1:00 - 2:30)

The story follows **Kosei Arima**, a piano prodigy who lost his ability to hear the sound of his own playing after the death of his mother. To him, the world was monotone—just like a sheet of black and white music. He was living in a world of silence and "gray."

Then came **Kaori Miyazono**, a free-spirited violinist. Unlike Kosei, who played music with mechanical precision, Kaori played with her soul. She was colorful, unpredictable, and radiant. She "forced" her way into Kosei’s world and dragged him back onto the stage.

Through her, Kosei learned that music isn't about being perfect; it’s about reaching people’s hearts. She turned his world from a dull "gray" into a vibrant "technicolor."

### III. The Meaning of the "Lie" (2:30 - 3:45)

The title asks a question: **What was the lie?** _(Pause for effect)_

Throughout the series, we think the lie is something simple. But in the final episode, through a letter written before her death, Kaori reveals her secret. She knew her time was running out due to illness. She told a "lie" to get closer to the person she had admired since childhood—Kosei.  
在整个系列中，我们一直认为谎言很简单。但在最终集，通过她死前写的一封信，香织揭示了她的秘密。她知道自己因病的时间不多了。她撒了一个“谎”，以接近她从小仰慕的人——光生。

She lied about her feelings, she lied about her health, and she put on a mask of bravery just to give Kosei the courage to play again. It was a "white lie" born out of selfless love. It was a lie that saved a life while hers was fading away.

### IV. Philosophical Reflection (3:45 - 4:30)

Why does this story resonate with us so much? I think it’s because it captures the essence of **youth**.

Youth is fleeting, just like cherry blossoms. We all face moments of "gray" in our lives—maybe it’s academic pressure, a loss of passion, or the fear of failure. But _Your Lie in April_ teaches us that even in our darkest moments, someone or something—a friend, a hobby, or a dream—can bring the color back.

It reminds us of the famous line from the show:

> "Even in the depths of the darkest oceans, some light always pierces through."

### V. Conclusion (4:30 - 5:00)

In conclusion, April is a season of beauty, but it is also a season of fleeting moments. Kaori Miyazono lived her life to the fullest, even though she knew it would be short.

As we enjoy this beautiful April, I hope we can all find our "color." Don't be afraid to take risks, don't be afraid to love, and most importantly, let music—or whatever you love—resonate in your heart.

A spring without her is coming, but the music she left behind will play forever.

**Thank you for listening.**
