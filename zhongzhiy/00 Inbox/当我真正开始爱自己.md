
## 《当我开始真正爱自己》

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我才明白，我的痛苦和情感折磨，

只是在提醒我：我的生活违背了自己的本心。

今天我明白了，这叫做“真实”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I found that anguish and emotional suffering are only warning signs that I was living against my own truth.
> 
> Today, I know, this is **"AUTHENTICITY"**.

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我开始了解，把自己的愿望强加给别人，

是多么地无礼。即便我知道时机尚未成熟，那个人也还没有准备好，

哪怕那个人就是我自己。

今天我明白了，这叫做“尊重”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I understood how much it can offend somebody as I try to force my desires on this person, even though I knew the time was not right and the person was not ready for it, even if this person was me.
> 
> Today, I call it **"RESPECT"**.

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我不再渴望过别人的生活，

我看到身边的每件事，都包含着成长的邀请。

今天我明白了，这叫做“成熟”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I stopped craving for a different life, and I could see that everything that surrounded me was inviting me to grow.
> 
> Today, I call it **"MATURITY"**.

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我才明白，在任何情况下，

我都在正确的时间、正确的地点，一切都发生得恰到好处。

因此我可以安心。

今天我明白了，这叫做“自信”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I understood that at any circumstance, I am in the right place at the right time, and everything happens at the exactly right moment. So I could be calm.
> 
> Today I call it **"SELF-CONFIDENCE"**.

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我不再牺牲自己的自由时间，

不再去勾勒什么宏伟的明天。

今天我只做有趣和快乐的事，做我热爱、让内心欢喜的事，

用我自己的方式，依循我自己的节奏。

今天我明白了，这叫做“单纯”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I quit stealing my own time, and I stopped designing huge projects for the future.
> 
> Today, I only do what brings me joy and happiness, things I love to do and that make my heart cheer, and I do them in my own way and in my own rhythm.
> 
> Today, I call it **"SIMPLICITY"**.

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我开始逃离所有不健康的东西：

食物、人、书籍、游戏，以及任何让我陷入低谷的人事物。

从前我把这叫做“利己主义”，

但今天我明白了，这叫做“自爱”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I freed myself of anything that is no good for my health – food, people, things, situations, and everything that drew me down and away from myself.
> 
> At first I called this attitude a healthy egoism.
> 
> Today I know it is **"LOVE OF ONESELF"**.

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我不再总想着要证明自己是对的，

这样一来，我犯的错误就更少了。

今天我明白了，这叫做“谦逊”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I quit trying to always be right, and since then I was less wrong.
> 
> Today I discovered **"MODESTY"**.

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我不再沉迷于过去，也不再为未来焦虑。

现在我只活在此时此刻，也就是一切正在发生的地方。

如今我天天这样生活，这就叫做“完美”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I refused to go on living in the past and worry about the future.
> 
> Now, I only live for the moment, where EVERYTHING is happening.
> 
> Today I live each day, day by day, and I call it **"FULFILLMENT"**.

### 当我开始真正爱自己，

我发现自己的理性会让我受挫甚至生病。

但当它与我的心灵相连，理性便成了我重要的盟友。

今天我把这种连接叫做“心的智慧”。

> **As I began to love myself,**
> 
> I recognized that my mind can torment me and it can make me ill. But as I connected it with my heart, my mind became a valuable ally.
> 
> Today I call this connection **"WISDOM OF THE HEART"**.

### 我们无须再害怕冲突、对抗

或者我们自己与他人的问题。

因为哪怕是星星，有时也会彼此碰撞，而在碰撞中，新的世界便诞生了。

今天我知道了：**这就是“生活”！**

> We no longer need to fear arguments, confrontations or any kind of problems with ourselves or others.
> 
> Even stars collide, and out of their crashing new worlds are born.
> 
> Today I know **"THAT IS LIFE"**!
